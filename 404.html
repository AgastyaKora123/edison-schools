Which district?

Start typing your school district's name. This app reads the information each district publishes itself, so it works for any district whose website is built the same way.

Which schools are yours?

Pick every school your children go to. Then this app can tell you whether they have school tomorrow, and what time they get out.

Ask about your schools.

Hours, closings, early dismissals, breaks, absences, buses, meals and policies — answered from the district's own published information. Works offline.
