Privacy

School Answers · last updated 21 November 2026

This app collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no server of ours. Nothing you type and nothing you choose is ever sent anywhere.

What stays on your phone

Three things, kept only in your own browser or app storage:

  • Which district you picked.
  • Which schools your children attend.
  • A copy of that district's calendar, so the app still answers with no signal.

Clearing your browser data, or deleting the app, removes all of it. We cannot see any of it and could not retrieve it if you asked.

What the app fetches

To answer, the app reads information your school district already publishes openly on the internet — its calendar and its announcements. Those requests go directly from your phone to the service your district's website runs on. They carry no name, no account and no identifier for you, only the district's own public reference number.

That service will see the ordinary things any website sees when a browser visits it, such as an address on the internet. We neither receive nor store that.

Your questions never leave the phone

What you type is worked out on your own device, against the calendar already stored there. It is not sent anywhere, not to us and not to anyone else, and no record of it is kept beyond the screen you are looking at.

There is one situation where that is not true, and it is not the version you installed: if this app is opened inside Claude, it can hand your question to Claude to word the answer, and that goes to Anthropic under Anthropic's privacy policy. The app you installed from an app store, or added to your home screen, does not do this and cannot.

Children

This app is for parents and carers, not for children, and it asks for no personal information from anyone. It holds no names, no ages, no photographs and no school records — only which schools you ticked, which is a setting on your own phone.

Advertising and selling

There is no advertising in this app, there are no trackers, and there is nothing to sell. No data about you is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Who made this

This is an unofficial app made by a parent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any school district. For same-day closings, your district's own notifications and website are the official word.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it.
